Do you have a project that demonstrates public voice and engagement around AI that should be included in the PAVE Case Book?

The case book includes entries:

  • Mapping engagement – showcasing outputs from dialogue, deliberation and participatory processes across the world.
  • Describing methods – exploring approaches for inclusive, informed and impactful public participation on AI.
  • Sharing learning and good practices – demonstrating how public voices can shape the future of AI.

How can you contribute?

If you have been involved in running or researching a public engagement project that explored the development, governance or use of artificial intelligence, anywhere in the world, we want to hear from you.

  • Nominate a project that our research team should document
  • Share a full case study of a project you have been involved in
What happens with contributions?

Submissions are reviewed by the PAVE Team.

  • For nominations, we may reach out to ask you for more details, or may carry out desk-research to populate case study details;
  • For full submissions, we will review your entry and suggest any editorial updates.

After submissions have been reviewed by the team, they may be included in the PAVE Case Book as:

  • Mapping entries – providing basic Who, What, Where information about a public voice and engagement project
  • Full cases – including more detailed information about the methodology and outputs from the project
  • Featured cases – selected to represent a range of approaches and communities engaged through public engagement on AI

If your submission is not included, we will write to let you know.

The PAVE Case Book forms a part of the Citizens' Track on AI, and we will be seeking opportunities over the coming year to showcase examples of public engagement from the case book – and to carry out analysis and synthesis of messages from included cases. This builds on a prototype showcase of the PAVE Case Book at AI for Good 2026.

About the team

The Public AI Voices & Engagement (PAVE) Case Book project is supported and co-hosted by the University of Sheffield’s Centre for Machine Intelligence, Ada Lovelace Institute, Connected by Data, the Citizens Track on AI, ISWE Foundation & partners.

The project is led by Dr Susan Oman, Tim Davies and Octavia Field-Reid and supported by a team including Dr Emily Rempel, Canning Malkin, Dr Erin McCloskey and Dr Emrys Schoemaker.

Data Protection Statement

All information submitted by this form will be held by Connected by Data according to our data protection policy. Access to the database containing submissions may be shared with approved project partners on the basis of signed data processing agreements.

With the exception of personal contact information, we may publish any of the information submitted to the PAVE casebook as part of the PAVE Website, printed resources or expo displays. We may make edits to information provided.

By submitting original content or analysis through this form you grant Connected by Data and its partners permission to use and re-use that content, in part or in full.

Information on cases may be published as part of an open dataset, under an open license.

You may choose during your submission whether or not you wish to be acknowledged as a contributor. You can let us know at any time if you want to change how you are acknowledged in materials going forward.

If you upload photos in your submission, you must confirm that the appropriate consents are in place for those photos to be republished. We will only use images in association with the case study they relate to unless otherwise agreed in writing.

If you upload documents as part of your submission, you agree that these may be republished as part of the PAVE website. Please ask us if you would prefer that we do not upload, and instead link to original copies on project websites, and we will endeavour to do this. Note that links to the original documents can be made available by way of a citation.
